# Immifile

Immifile is an independent USCIS case tracker. It helps people save receipt numbers, record the updates they receive, and open official government tools.

- [Privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})
- [Support]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }})

Immifile is not affiliated with USCIS, DHS, DOJ, or the U.S. government and does not provide legal advice.
